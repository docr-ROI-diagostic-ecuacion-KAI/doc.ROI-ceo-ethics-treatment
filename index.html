What the Ethical Code covers

The code sets rules for people and organisations; AI agents and systems; models, algorithms and training; data and flows; and derived outputs. It applies across the full lifecycle, from design to commercialisation.

Its practical purpose is to expand human capability with clear accountability, controlled data and auditable decisions.

Productivity commitment: Efficiency must not be used to dismiss people. Reinvest released hours in employee development and commercial or operational growth.
Adherence: the source makes participation conditional on adherence and asks signatories to extend that commitment to third parties using AI. It proposes contractual annexes and actions for non-compliance. This tool records draft status; it does not determine legal validity or impose sanctions.

How the treatment works

1 · Select. Choose up to four values and review the eight DEEP principles.

2 · Define. Turn each value into an observable commitment.

3 · Measure. Set an implementation KPI and record effort and evidence.

4 · Connect. Prepare candidate signals for SWOT, Goals & Budget and C-Level review. Proposals need evidence.

5 · Validate. Value, verify, then validate. The status turns blue only after all three review steps are complete.
